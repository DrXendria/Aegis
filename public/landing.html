Zero-Knowledge Cryptographic Flow

1

Master Password Derivation

Your master password is hashed in-memory via PBKDF2 SHA-256 with 600,000 rounds + a unique cryptographic salt. It never travels over any network.

2

Local AES-256-GCM Encryption

The resulting 256-bit key encrypts your secrets locally on your processor with an unrepeatable 96-bit Initialization Vector (IV).

3

Encrypted Cloud Synchronization

Only the opaque ciphertext binary blob is stored in Firestore. Aegis servers, Firebase engineers, and cloud databases can never inspect or recover your plaintext data.

Mathematical Guarantee: Even in the event of a catastrophic database leak, all stored entries remain indistinguishable from pseudo-random noise.
